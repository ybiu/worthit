# WorthIt product brief

## The question

AI-assisted work is easy to produce and hard to evaluate. A completed task can still be low-value; an unfinished prototype can already be worth the investment. WorthIt makes that judgement explicit without pretending incomplete data is precise.

## Product promise

Given the context for one work unit, WorthIt produces a concise, evidence-led answer to: **was this work worth its AI, human, and other costs?**

The answer is comparable across coding, research, writing, prototypes, and operations because it keeps the same cost categories, evidence labels, score scale, verdict vocabulary, and JSON contract.

## Principles

1. **Outcome before activity.** A long session, many tokens, or a polished draft is not proof of value.
2. **Evidence before precision.** Facts, reports, estimates, and unknowns remain visibly different.
3. **One work unit at a time.** Broad initiatives are split so the judgement remains meaningful.
4. **Qualitative is legitimate.** Monetary ROI is used only when the benefit estimate has a defensible basis.
5. **Useful uncertainty.** Missing evidence should lead to the next measurement, not a fabricated score.

## Boundaries

WorthIt is a decision aid and record format, not accounting software, a billing collector, or an automated token meter. It does not access provider accounts or require secrets. Its assessments should inform a human decision, especially where financial, hiring, safety, or policy consequences are material.
